TrackMe 1.8.9

- Five new designs for sharing a ride: the route on its own, a data card, a transparent sticker for your own photos, one coloured by the light you rode in, and one that appears only when you beat a record.
- Pick several rides in History and share them as one picture — a journey with its stops named, or every route on one ground.
- Place names on an export are looked up only when you ask for them, and kept on your phone.
- Steadier recording when a ride is mostly stop-start.
- What's new now has a header, keeps its formatting, and marks itself unread until you have read it.
