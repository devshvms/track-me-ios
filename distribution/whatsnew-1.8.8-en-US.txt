TrackMe 1.8.8

- Rebuilt distance and pace estimation. Walking and running are measured more accurately, pauses are detected more reliably, and stretches with no GPS are shown as gaps rather than guessed at.
- Routes no longer draw a straight line through a section the app could not see.
- Home cards explain themselves without pushing the page around, and Live sharing now says who can see your location.
- Start is enabled once a second person is in your group, and group errors are explained in plain language.
